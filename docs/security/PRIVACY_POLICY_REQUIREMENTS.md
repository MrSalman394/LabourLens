# Privacy Policy, Ethics & Worker Data Protection

## 1. Ethical Principles for AI Monitoring

Workplace surveillance systems can infringe on worker dignity if implemented without clear boundaries. LabourLens is engineered strictly around **Safety & Operational Verification**, explicitly rejecting punitive personal surveillance.

### Core Ethical Pillars:
1. **No Invasive Biometrics**: No emotion detection, sentiment analysis, voice eavesdropping, or keystroke tracking.
2. **Strict Spatial Boundaries**: Camera analytics run exclusively within designated industrial production zones. Restrooms, breakrooms, dining areas, and prayer spaces are strictly blacklisted from camera coverage.
3. **Transparent Worker Disclosures**: Workers are notified via the mobile app regarding what data is collected, why it is processed, and how long it is stored.

---

## 2. Regulatory Compliance (GDPR & Labor Law)

### 2.1 Right to Access & Correction (GDPR Art. 15/16)
- Workers have access to their full attendance logs, shift history, and safety incident tickets directly within the mobile application.
- Disputed attendance records can be flagged for supervisor review.

### 2.2 Data Minimization & Retention Limits (GDPR Art. 5)
- **High-Resolution CCTV Video**: Raw stream buffers are overwritten after 72 hours.
- **Annotated Violation Snapshots**: Preserved for 90 days for safety review, then automatically purged by automated cleanup scripts unless tied to an active legal/regulatory OSHA inquiry.
- **Automated Face Redaction**: Archived recordings automatically apply Gaussian blurring to all worker faces not involved in an incident.
